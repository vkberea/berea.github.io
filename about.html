About Bertos AI

We're on a mission to help Dubai's healthcare clinics never miss another patient inquiry.

Our Story

Bertos AI was founded with a simple observation: Dubai's dental and aesthetic clinics were losing patients every single night. Not because of poor service or high prices — but because no one was there to answer when patients reached out.

We watched as clinics invested heavily in marketing, beautiful facilities, and skilled practitioners — only to see potential patients slip away to competitors who happened to respond first. The data was clear: 78% of patients book with the first clinic to respond.

"We realized that the problem wasn't the clinics — it was the technology available to them. Enterprise AI solutions cost hundreds of thousands of dirhams. Small clinics were left behind. We decided to change that."

Today, Bertos AI provides intelligent, bilingual AI assistants that work around the clock — answering patient questions, booking appointments, and ensuring no inquiry goes unanswered. We've made enterprise-grade technology accessible to clinics of all sizes.
