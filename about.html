About Bertos

We're on a mission to ensure Dubai's healthcare clinics never lose another patient.

Our Story

Bertos was born from a simple observation: Dubai's dental and aesthetic clinics were losing patients every single night. Not because of poor service or high prices — but because no one was there to answer when patients reached out.

Clinics invest heavily in marketing, beautiful facilities, and skilled practitioners — only to see potential patients slip away to competitors who happen to respond first. The data is clear: 78% of patients book with the first clinic to respond.

"The problem wasn't the clinics — it was the technology available to them. Enterprise AI solutions cost hundreds of thousands of dirhams. Small and medium clinics were left behind. That had to change."

Today, Bertos provides intelligent, bilingual AI assistants that work around the clock — answering patient questions, booking appointments, and ensuring no inquiry goes unanswered. We've made enterprise-grade technology accessible to clinics of all sizes across Dubai.
