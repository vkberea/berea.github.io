The Challenge

Their challenge was unique: while most AI solutions worked well in English, they struggled to find one that could handle conversational Arabic — including Gulf dialect variations.

Their reception team included only one Arabic-speaking staff member, creating a bottleneck for Arabic inquiries, especially during peak hours and after the clinic closed.

The Solution

Bertos developed a custom Arabic-first AI assistant that was specifically trained on Gulf Arabic patterns, cultural communication norms, and Islamic calendar considerations (such as Ramadan timing adjustments). The assistant could:

  • Communicate naturally in both Modern Standard Arabic and Gulf dialect
  • Understand and respond appropriately to common Arabic expressions and greetings
