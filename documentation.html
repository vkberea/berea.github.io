Getting Started

Welcome to Bertos

Congratulations on joining Bertos! Your AI assistant is designed to help your clinic capture more patient inquiries, respond instantly 24/7, and book more appointments — all while maintaining your clinic's professional voice.

This documentation will guide you through:

  • Initial setup — Connecting your WhatsApp and/or Instagram accounts
  • Customization — Teaching the AI about your clinic's services, pricing, and policies
  • Going live — Testing and launching your AI assistant
  • Optimization — Using analytics to improve performance over time

💡 Tip: Most clinics are fully operational within 3-5 business days of onboarding.

What You'll Need

Before we begin setup, please have the following ready:

  • WhatsApp Business Account — A phone number dedicated to your clinic (can be your existing clinic number)
  • Instagram Business Account — If you want Instagram DM automation (Professional plan and above)
  • Clinic Information — Services list, pricing, opening hours, location, and common patient questions
  • Admin Access — You'll need to grant Bertos access to your WhatsApp Business and Instagram accounts

⚠️ Important: WhatsApp requires a verified business phone number. If you're using a new number, verification can take 24-48 hours.

Onboarding Timeline

Here's what to expect during your onboarding:

Day 1-2: Information Gathering

  • Complete our clinic questionnaire
  • Provide services list and pricing
  • Share common patient questions and preferred answers

Day 2-3: AI Configuration

  • We configure your AI with your clinic's information
  • Set up conversation flows for common scenarios
  • Configure booking and handoff rules

Day 3-4: Testing

  • You receive access to test the AI
  • Review and provide feedback on responses
  • We make adjustments based on your input

Day 5: Go Live

  • Final approval from you
  • AI assistant goes live on your channels
  • We monitor closely for the first 48 hours

WhatsApp Business Setup

Prerequisites

Before connecting WhatsApp, ensure you have:

  • A WhatsApp Business Account (not regular WhatsApp)
  • A verified phone number for your clinic
  • Access to the phone to receive a verification code
  • A Facebook Business Manager account (we'll help you set this up if needed)

💡 Tip: You can use your existing clinic WhatsApp number. The AI will handle messages alongside your team, or you can configure it to handle all messages.

Step 1: Connect Your Account

Your Bertos account manager will send you a secure connection link. Here's what happens:

  1. Click the connection link in your email
  2. Log in to your Facebook Business Manager
  3. Select your WhatsApp Business Account
  4. Grant Bertos permission to send and receive messages
  5. Verify your phone number if prompted

The entire process takes about 5-10 minutes.

⚠️ Important: Only grant access through official links from your Bertos account manager. We will never ask for your WhatsApp password.

Step 2: Configure Message Templates

WhatsApp requires pre-approved templates for certain message types. We'll set up templates for:

  • Appointment Confirmations — Sent when a patient books
  • Appointment Reminders — Sent 24 hours before appointments
  • Follow-up Messages — Re-engage patients who didn't book

Template approval typically takes 24-48 hours. We handle the submission process for you.

Step 3: Test Your Connection

Once connected, test your setup:

  1. Send a WhatsApp message to your clinic number from a personal phone
  2. You should receive an automated response within seconds
  3. Try asking about services, pricing, and appointment availability
  4. Test the human handoff by asking to speak to a person

💡 Tip: Test in both English and Arabic (if bilingual is enabled) to ensure both languages work correctly.

Instagram Setup

Prerequisites

Instagram automation requires:

  • An Instagram Business or Creator Account
  • Your Instagram account must be connected to a Facebook Page
  • You must have admin access to the Facebook Page
  • Professional or Premium Bertos plan

If your Instagram isn't connected to Facebook, follow these steps:

  1. Open Instagram → Settings → Account
  2. Tap "Linked Accounts"
  3. Select Facebook and connect your Page

Connecting Your Account

The connection process is similar to WhatsApp:

  1. Click the Instagram connection link from your account manager
  2. Log in to Facebook Business Manager
  3. Select your Instagram account
  4. Grant Bertos permission to access messages

💡 Tip: Instagram automation works for DMs, story mentions, and comment replies (coming soon).

Story Mention Responses

When someone mentions your clinic in their Instagram story, the AI can automatically send them a thank-you DM. This is a powerful way to engage patients who are already advocating for your clinic.

Default story mention response:

"Thanks so much for the mention! 😊 We're thrilled you had a great experience. If you ever need anything or want to book your next appointment, just message us here!"

You can customize this message during onboarding.

Customizing Your AI

Response Style & Tone

Your AI assistant adopts your clinic's voice. During onboarding, you can specify:

  • Formality level — Professional, friendly, or casual
  • Emoji usage — None, minimal, or moderate
  • Response length — Concise or detailed
  • Greeting style — Your preferred welcome message

Example tone options:

Professional: "Thank you for contacting Marina Dental Clinic. I'd be happy to help you with information about our services."

Friendly: "Hi there! 👋 Thanks for reaching out to Marina Dental. How can I help you today?"

💡 Tip: Check your weekly analytics report for "unanswered questions" — these are great candidates for new FAQs.

Analytics & Reporting

Weekly Performance Reports

Every week, you'll receive a performance report including:

  • Total conversations — How many patients contacted you
  • Response time — Average time to first response
  • Resolution rate — % of conversations handled without human intervention
  • Booking rate — % of conversations that resulted in appointments
  • Peak hours — When patients are most active
  • Language breakdown — English vs Arabic conversations

Key Metrics Explained

Response Time: The time between a patient's message and the AI's first response. Target: under 10 seconds.

Resolution Rate: Percentage of conversations where the AI fully addressed the patient's needs without requiring human intervention. A healthy rate is 70-85%.

Handoff Rate: Percentage of conversations transferred to your human team. Some handoffs are expected and healthy (complex cases, complaints, etc.).

Booking Conversion: Percentage of conversations that result in a booked appointment. This is your most important metric for ROI calculation.

Calculating Your ROI

Here's how to calculate the return on your Bertos investment:

Step 1: Find your average patient value (total revenue ÷ number of patients)

Step 2: Multiply by new bookings from Bertos

Step 3: Subtract your monthly Bertos fee

Example: Average patient value: AED 1,500 New bookings this month: 25 Revenue from Bertos: 25 × 1,500 = AED 37,500 Monthly Bertos fee: AED 3,500 Net ROI: AED 34,000 (10.7x return)

Human Handoffs

When Does the AI Hand Off?

The AI is configured to hand off to your human team when:

  • A patient explicitly asks to speak to a person
  • The conversation involves a complaint or negative sentiment
  • The AI doesn't know the answer after 2 attempts
  • The patient has a medical emergency (AI directs to emergency services first)
  • Complex insurance or billing questions arise

You can customize these triggers based on your preferences.

How Handoffs Work

When a handoff is triggered:

  1. The AI informs the patient that a team member will respond shortly
  2. Your team receives a notification (WhatsApp, email, or both)
  3. The full conversation history is included for context
  4. Your team responds directly through WhatsApp/Instagram
  5. The AI stays silent until you indicate the conversation is resolved

💡 Tip: Set up a dedicated WhatsApp group or Slack channel for handoff notifications so nothing falls through the cracks.

Security & Privacy

Data Protection

We take data security seriously:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Access Control: Only authorized Bertos staff can access your data, and only when necessary for support
  • Data Location: All data is stored in secure cloud infrastructure
  • No Training on Your Data: We never use your patient conversations to train AI models

Compliance

Bertos is designed with healthcare compliance in mind:

  • Compliant with UAE data protection regulations
  • Patient data is never shared with third parties for marketing
  • You retain full ownership of your patient data
  • Data deletion available upon request

For detailed information, see our Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

By default, conversation data is retained for:

  • Active conversations: Indefinitely while you're a client
  • Analytics data: 24 months
  • After cancellation: 30 days, then permanently deleted

You can request earlier deletion at any time by contacting your account manager.

Troubleshooting

AI Not Responding

If your AI isn't responding to messages:

  1. Check your connection status in your Bertos dashboard (if available) or contact support
  2. Verify WhatsApp Business is still connected to your Facebook Business Manager
  3. Test from a different phone to rule out issues with a specific device
  4. Check for WhatsApp outages at status.whatsapp.com

If the issue persists, contact support immediately.

AI Giving Wrong Information

If the AI is providing incorrect information:

  1. Screenshot the conversation showing the incorrect response
  2. Note what the correct answer should be
  3. Email your account manager with both pieces of information
  4. We'll update the AI within 24 hours

💡 Tip: If it's urgent (e.g., wrong pricing being quoted), call or WhatsApp our support line for immediate assistance.

Not Receiving Handoff Notifications

If you're missing handoff notifications:

  • Check your spam/junk folder for email notifications
  • Verify the notification phone number/email is correct in your settings
  • Ensure WhatsApp notifications are enabled on your device
  • Check if your team's phones have Do Not Disturb enabled

Contact support to verify your notification settings are configured correctly.
